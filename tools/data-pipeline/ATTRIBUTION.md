# Terrain imagery attribution

The macro terrain imagery is derived from NASA Earth Observatory Blue Marble,
August 2004. Source image:

https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/august/world.topo.bathy.200408.3x21600x10800.jpg

NASA content is generally not subject to copyright in the United States. The
generated `terrain-imagery.jpg` is a Mercator reprojection and crop covering
the scene bounds declared in `scene-manifest.json`.
